Model Architecture & Parameters

Subscriber Risk Directory (CSV)

What it contains: Complete records for all 5,000 subscribers, including model churn probabilities, risk levels, engagement scores, monthly fees, and watch hours.

Business Purpose: Import directly into your CRM (HubSpot, Braze, Salesforce) to trigger automated win-back email workflows.

Retention Action Audit Trail (CSV)

What it contains: All scheduled and completed retention initiatives stored in the database, including action type, subscriber ID, staff notes, and status.

Business Purpose: Weekly operational review to audit team intervention volume, follow-up SLAs, and campaign efficacy.

Model Governance & Evaluation (CSV)

What it contains: Formal model evaluation scores (Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1, ROC-AUC), confusion matrix counts, and Gini feature rankings.

Business Purpose: Machine Learning governance documentation and stakeholder validation reporting.
